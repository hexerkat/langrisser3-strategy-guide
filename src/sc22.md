## Walkthrough

A few turns in, hydras and sphinxes appear at the bottom-right and **Bahamut** at
the top-left; Emerick then orders a **retreat**, and the monsters turn on his
troops. You *could* wait for the two enemy sides to grind each other down, but
**Bahamut is painfully slow** — it's far faster to cut down Emerick yourself, then
deal with Bahamut on your own terms.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Opening       Engage Emerick's Imperial line.
 Monsters      Bahamut (top-left) + hydras/sphinxes
               (bottom-right) arrive; Emerick retreats.
 Finish        Kill Emerick, then take Bahamut yourself.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
