# Scenario 07 — Mountain Pass Reunion
*A meeting both nostalgic and sorrowful*

> {{enf:scen054:21}}

On the road to the Lushiris Gate, Diehärte meets **Freya** in arms against him —
the first of the duels that decide her fate. Freya is **essential for the best
ending**, so how you fight her matters as much as whether you win.

## Walkthrough

Freya sits behind a wall of troops, but the formation cracks if you advance
slowly and exploit the unit triangle. **Leave an enemy or two standing** — wiping
out every unit drops Freya's love index, and you want it high.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Advance       Push in slowly; use cavalry/infantry/spear
               matchups instead of brute force.
 Persuade      Move Diehärte adjacent to Freya, or simply
               stand beside her and let her strike first —
               both open a persuasion scene.
 Avoid         Do NOT have Diehärte attack Freya, and do
               NOT clear every enemy (each is Freya −1).
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
