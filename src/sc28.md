## Walkthrough

You begin **surrounded** — sphinxes, master dinos, iron golems and minotaurs press
in from every side. Don't rush; **advance slowly** and post **archers and spearmen
on your flanks** to bring down the sphinxes and master dinos, which are the worst
of the swarm. Carve a lane to Grove and finish him.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Surrounded    Hold formation; guard both flanks.
 Flanks        Archers/spears down the sphinx & dinos.
 Finish        Push through to Grove.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
