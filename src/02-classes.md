# Classes & Class Change

Every commander can **change class** as they level. Each character belongs to an
**attribute line** (Infantry, Cavalry, Mage…); leveling within a class — or using
a **Divine Blessing** — promotes them up that line. Reach the top and the right
item can open a **hidden class**, which usually grants powerful new skills.

> The hero and Holy Beast Jugler are special: various **Runes** add attributes to
> them regardless of level (see the Items chapter).

## Class ladders & hidden classes

Each attribute promotes **base → mid → top** (the mercenaries you can hire at
each step are in parentheses). A **hidden class-up item** then opens a special
class for the listed characters — skills it grants are noted in brackets.

### Infantry
`Fighter → Gladiator → Swordsman` *(hire: Soldier · Infantry · Legion)*
> ★ **Hero's Stone** → Diehärte (**Hero**) · Lewin (**King** — adds
> metal-armor skill) · Kirikaze (**Samurai Master** — Focus, Iai, Self-Destruct,
> Critical) · Altemüller (**Emperor** — hire Grenadier) · Varna (**Queen**)

### Spear
`Lord → High Lord → General` *(hire: Pike · Halberd · Phalanx)*
> ★ **Scathach Tome** → Lewin · Gilbert · Pierre (**Marshal** — adds shield &
> spear equip skills)

### Marine
`Croco Knight → Serpent Knight → Serpent Lord` *(hire: Merman · Lizardman · Archelon)*
> ★ **Mermaid Tear** → Diehärte · Pierre · Dios (**Serpent Master** — hire Nixie)

### Bow
`Hunter → Archer → Sniper` *(hire: Crossbow · Elf · Ballista)*
> ★ **Apollo Arrow** → Silver Wolf (**Ranger** — hire High Elf) · Varna
> (**Ranger** — hire Jäger)

### Flight
`Hawk Knight (Pegasus Knight) → Dragon Knight → Dragon Lord` *(hire: Harpy · Fairy · Griffon)*
> ★ **Heavenly Wings** → Diehärte · Luna (**Dragon Master** — hire Angel)

### Cavalry
`Knight → Silver Knight → Highlander` *(hire: Trooper · Lancer · Dragoon)*
> ★ **Knight Crest** → Gilbert (**Knight Master**) · Emerick / Altemüller
> (**Royal Guard** — hire Royal Lancer)

### Unicorn *(special cavalry)*
`Unicorn Knight → Gran Knight → Gran Lord` *(hire: Unicorn · Sleipnir · Chimera)*
> ★ **Ancient Egg** → Luna / Freya (**Gran Master**)

### Mounted Bow
`Archer Knight → Bow Knight → Bow Lord` *(hire: Centaur · Angoramora · Hapilsag)*
> ★ **Arrow of Fate** → Dios (**Bow Master**)

### Thief
`Bandit → Thief (adds Swift) → Assassin (adds Hide, Critical)` *(hire: Beggar · Rogue)*
> ★ **Secret Manual** → Silver Wolf (**High Master** — adds reflex-up) · Kirikaze
> (**Ninja** — adds ninja skills, hire Kunoichi)

### Priest-Warrior
`Bishop → Paladin → Saint` *(hire: Guardman · Monk · Crusader)*
> ★ **Holy Symbol** → Diehärte · Tiaris · Sophia · Fauvel (**Sage** — adds First
> Aid, Chain Magic) · Freya (**Princess** — adds First Aid)

### Cleric
`Cleric → Priest → High Priest` *(hire: Guardman · Monk)*
> ★ **White Grimoire** → Sophia · Jessica (**Agent**)

### Mage
`Sorcerer → Mage → Archmage` *(hire: Soldier)*
> ★ **Magic Symbol** → Diehärte · Liffany · Fauvel · Jessica (**Hermit** — adds
> Chain Magic, magic-tool equip)

### Heretic
`Shaman → Summoner → Zaverer` *(hire: Soldier)*
> ★ **Black Grimoire** → Tiaris · Liffany (**Wizard** — adds magic-tool equip)

## Special tree — Do Kahni (the Shika tribe)

The Brave Do Kahni climbs a unique Shika-tribe ladder:

| Line | Ladder *(hire)* |
|---|---|
| Spear | `Spear → Shika Spear → Shika Shika Spear` *(Bamboo)* — ★ **Scathach Tome** → **Shika Marshal** |

| Marine | `Swimmer → Pirate → Captain` *(Shika Frog · Shika Shark · Shika Orca)* |
| Infantry | `Fighter → Gladiator → Swordsman` *(Barbarian · Berserker · Shielder)* |

## Special tree — Holy Beast Jugler (monster classes)

Jugler transforms into beasts rather than soldiers:

| Line | Ladder *(hire)* |
|---|---|
| Unicorn | `Manticore → Cerberus → Master Dino` *(Hellhound · Sabertiger · Bone Dino)* — ★ **Ancient Egg** → **Behemoth** |
| Infantry | `Werewolf → Troll → Minotaur` *(Wolfman · Ogre)* — adds Regenerate, Berserk |

| Flight | `Wurm → Wyvern → Sphinx` *(Harpy · Bat · Gremlin)* — ★ **Heaven's Wings** → **Roc** (hire Gargoyle) |
