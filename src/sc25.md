## Walkthrough

The demons near you batter **Olver's door with magic** while the rest charge.
Reach his house and Olver offers to blow the doors open with **meteor**. Answer
**"yes"** to let him (or "no" to stop him). Once the two doors fall, you choose
where Olver stays: the **1st option keeps him put**, the **2nd sends him to the
bottom** of the map. Choose with care, because **if Olver dies, it's game over**. Clear
the field and you walk away with **Langrisser**, and Jessica joins.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Defend        Demons blast Olver's door; reach the house.
 Olver's door  "Yes" → he meteors his own doors open.
 Place Olver   Pick his position (stay / go bottom).
               Keep him alive or it's game over.
 Reward        Langrisser at the end; Jessica joins.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

## Branching dialogue

The decisions are Olver's door (yes/no) and his position (stay / move). See the
walkthrough. After this scenario, a Sc 26+ save unlocks **Stage ?5** via the
scenario-select.
