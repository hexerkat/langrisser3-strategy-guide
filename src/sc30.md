## Walkthrough

Ruud's troops engage you head-on, but **two dragon lords and two assassins peel off
toward the water gate**. Send a detachment to **guard the gate**, since its
destruction is an instant loss. Once most of Ruud's force is down, the **Dark
Knight** and his retinue join the fight.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Split off     Spare units to defend the water gate from
               the dragon lords + assassins.
 Main          Grind down Ruud's column.
 Dark Knight   Appears once Ruud's force is broken.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

## Branching dialogue

Having **Dios persuade Ruud** yields the **{{en:fntsys10:30}}** (and spares an old
comrade). The "mask" comes off here, and the Dark Knight's identity is laid bare.
