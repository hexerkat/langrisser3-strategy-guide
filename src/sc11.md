# Scenario 11 — Deception
*The bewitching woman in the woods*

> {{enf:scen062:26}}

A frightened "civilian" begs your escort through the forest — but she is the demon
**Feraquea** in disguise, and the trap springs the moment you cross the bridge.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Victory** | Get the NPC to safety → then defeat **Feraquea** once she reveals herself |
| **Defeat** | Diehärte falls · the NPC dies |

## Walkthrough

You must keep the "civilian" alive — **her death is an instant loss**, even though
you already suspect what she is. Escort her toward the bridge; once across, she
transforms into **Feraquea** and fresh demons rise from the river at the bottom
of the map. Only then can you turn on her. Play it through to the reveal rather
than trying to end it early.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Escort        Shield the "civilian"; her death = defeat.
 Bridge        Crossing it triggers her change into
               Feraquea; sea serpents and bats spawn.
 Finish        Now defeat Feraquea.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
