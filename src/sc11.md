# Scenario 11: The Enchantress
*The bewitching woman in the woods*

> {{enf:scen062:26}}

A frightened "civilian" begs your escort through the forest, but she is the demon
**Feraquea** in disguise, and the trap springs as soon as you reach her.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Victory** | Get the NPC to safety → then defeat **Feraquea** once she reveals herself |
| **Defeat** | Diehärte falls · the NPC dies (*listed, but her death does not end the game*) |

## Walkthrough

The "civilian" is the trap. Her death is listed as a loss, but **it does not end
the game**, so there is no need to rush to her. Hold the near side of the bridge
and meet the monsters there. When you reach her, or her HP runs out, she
transforms into **Feraquea**, the goal becomes defeating her, and fresh demons
rise from the river at the bottom of the map.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Hold          Fight the monsters at the bridge; the
               "civilian" dying does not end the game.
 Reveal        She becomes Feraquea; sea serpents and
               bats spawn downriver.
 Finish        Now defeat Feraquea.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
