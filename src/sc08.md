# Scenario 08: The Southern Lushiris Gate
*Brave Do Kahni bars the way*

> {{enf:scen056:19}}

The ancient Lushiris Gate once held back the monsters; now, with the barrier
silent, Diehärte hurries to restore it, only to find **Do Kahni** lying in wait.

## Walkthrough

Race for the **small isle** ringed by the river, where Do Kahni stands. He casts
a fire spell that sends a **wall of flame sweeping left-to-right** across the
whole map, scorching everything in the open. The water around the isle shields
you, so get your units onto it before the fire rolls through.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Rush          Move toward Do Kahni's river isle fast.
 Fire wall     His spell sends flame across the map.
               Stand on the water-ringed isle to be safe.
 Finish        Cut down Do Kahni to win.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

## Branching dialogue

None (the item trigger above is the only choice).
