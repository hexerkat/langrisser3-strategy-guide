## Walkthrough

The **serpent knights spring the moment you touch the water**, so move fast and on
your terms. **Dios is badly outnumbered** and will try to break for the water. He
is strong but cannot hold against the whole enemy line, so relieve the pressure on
him early. He joins after the battle.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Water         Touching it wakes the serpent knights.
               Choose your moment.
 Save Dios     Relieve him before he is swarmed.
 Persuade      Court Freya (see below); avoid attacking
               her directly.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
