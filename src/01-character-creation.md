# Character Creation: The Questions of Lushiris

Before the story begins, the goddess **Lushiris** tests the young hero with a
chain of questions. Your answers are more than flavor. They secretly shape
**Diehärte**'s whole growth: the unit type he becomes at each rank, his starting
stats and skills, a few bonus items, and even how close he starts to each
heroine.

> **The protagonist's default name is Diehärte.** If you rename him, every
> "Diehärte" below is simply your chosen name.

There are **two layers** to the quiz:

```
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │  LAYER 1 · Attribute path        Q1-Q11                     │
 │     Sets your unit type at Lv 1 / Lv 10 / Lv 30 / Lv 50.    │
 │                                                             │
 │  LAYER 2 · Personality           Q12-Q27                    │
 │     Stat boosts, learned skills, bonus items, heroine       │
 │     affinity. No more branching, just rewards.              │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Stat shorthand: **STR** strength · **DEF** defense · **HP** vitality ·
**INT** intelligence · **CHA** charisma · **ATK** attack-mod · **DMG** damage-mod ·
**MOV** move-mod · **Mercs** mercenary count · **Merc-HP** mercenary HP-regen ·
**MDef** magic resistance. Affinity bonuses raise that heroine's love index.
