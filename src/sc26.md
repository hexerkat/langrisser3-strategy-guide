## Walkthrough

Lead with **mages** to clear the dark snipers, which are the real nuisance, and
don't linger. Lewin and Diehärte get surrounded fast. **Kirikaze charges
straight for the throne room**, so support him before he's overwhelmed. When he
reaches Feraquea you may take control of him (**yes/no**).

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Snipe         Mages kill the dark snipers first.
 Tempo         Don't get surrounded; aid Kirikaze's rush.
 Kirikaze      Take control when he nears the throne.
 Finish        Down the pretender.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

## Branching dialogue

Take control of Kirikaze at the throne (**yes/no**). If **Kirikaze** strikes
Feraquea, all females +1.
