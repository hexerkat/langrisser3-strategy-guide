# Scenario 12 — The Wise Sage Fauvel
*A race against the clock at the north gate*

> {{enf:scen064:29}}

Past Feraquea, the party reaches the eastern gate to find an Imperial force —
and the strategist **Fauvel**, whom they must save before the clock runs out.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Victory** | Destroy all enemies **within 14 turns** *(then clear the reinforcements)* |
| **Defeat** | Diehärte falls · Fauvel falls · time runs out |
| **New ally** | **Fauvel** (joins after the battle) |

## Walkthrough

The 14-turn counter is the danger, and there's a trick to it: **kill the Lv 26
gladiator by the bridge** to stop the clock. Clear the opening force and
**Emerick** arrives at the bottom-left cliff (one of your start points) with two
dragon knights at mid-bottom. Fauvel joins once the dust settles.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Stop clock    Kill the Lv 26 gladiator near the bridge.
 Clear         Wipe the opening force.
 Emerick       Spawns bottom-left with dragon knights —
               regroup and finish.
 After battle  Fauvel joins the army.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
