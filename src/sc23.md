## Walkthrough

**Attacking Grove triggers a second wave** — a wyvern from the top-right, a hydra
from the bottom-right, two living armors by the rivers, the rest from your start.
**Thin the field first**, then commit to Grove so you aren't swarmed. As ever, do
not let all the civilians die — that is an instant loss.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Clear         Whittle down the demons; shield civilians.
 Trigger       Hitting Grove spawns the second wave.
 Finish        Bring everything down, Grove last.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
