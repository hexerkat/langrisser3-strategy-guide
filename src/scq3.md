# Stage ?3: The Queen and Aesthetic Men
*A flying doll, a queen, and some very aesthetic men*

> {{enf:scen118:19}}

A runaway doll leads the party on a long, absurd chase through Castle Velzeria,
ending before a queen's chamber. It is a tongue-in-cheek cameo stage.

## Unlocking ?3 (during Scenario 27)

Around the great blue crystal where Feraquea stands, place **Diehärte** on the
smaller blue stones in order (**right, then bottom, then left**), answering each
prompt with **"{{en:scen028:135}}"** ({{jp:scen028:135:おがむ}}),
**"{{en:scen028:128}}"** ({{jp:scen028:128:とりあえず踊る}}) and
**"{{en:scen028:119}}"** ({{jp:scen028:119:ポージングしてみる}}).

## Walkthrough

A comedy stage built around a **doll chase** to the queen's hall. Sweep up the
hidden items along the way, and once **Jessica takes the throne**, you gain the
option to recruit the ever-cheerful **Uminin**.

## Branching dialogue

The item prompts above are the decisions. The "汁" (broth) frame splits by your
answer to the age question.
