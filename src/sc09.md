## Walkthrough

The map is dense forest, so keep **cavalry out of the trees** (they fight poorly
there). Around turn 4-5 **Kah Shind summons a fog** that blocks *your* magic
while enemy shamans keep casting. Kill Kah Shind to clear it. Once every Shika
is down, you face **Emerick**, whose column arrives at the top on turn 7 and heads
for Kah Shind first; regroup before you
engage them.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Forest        Fight with infantry/spears; spare cavalry.
 Turn 4-5      Kah Shind's fog kills your spellcasting.
               Hunt him down to lift it.
 Turn 7        Emerick arrives at the top and goes for
               Kah Shind. Re-form, then finish them.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
