# Scenario 15 — The Sword of Decimation
*A vault of relics, and thieves racing for them*

> {{enf:scen070:21}}

The legendary **Sword of Decimation** lies in a treasure-vault stronghold — and a
band of thieves means to plunder it first. This is the richest map in the game;
grab everything before they flee with it.

## Walkthrough

The **thieves dash for the vaults and try to escape with whatever they grab**, so
this is a race — spread out and beat them to the treasure, above all the **central
vault** holding the Sword. Your start is **forested on one side** (bad for cavalry,
and enemy spearmen press you there early); the **narrow alley on the right** is
open ground, so send horsemen through it instead.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Race          Reach the vaults before the thieves loot
               and flee — the central Sword above all.
 Routing       Keep cavalry out of the forested side;
               use the right-hand alley.
 Finish        Clear every enemy to win.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
