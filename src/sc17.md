## Walkthrough

This is a cramped **urban battle** and Freya is buried in defenders. The enemy
**will not come out into the open** — they wait for you to push into the city, so
don't sit back. **Silver Wolf** arrives as an NPC to help and joins afterward.
Grind forward street by street and protect your casters in the tight lanes.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Push in       Enemies hold the city; you must advance.
 Silver Wolf   Joins the fight as an NPC ally.
 Reach Freya   Cut through to her — mind the persuasion
               choices below.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
