## Walkthrough

**Galshook fires a beam straight down the map** from its position to the bottom
edge, again and again until it is destroyed — and the beam **damages enemies as
well as your units**. The grass-field troops all advance on you from the opening,
so let Galshook's own fire thin them while you pick your approach, then converge
on Bortz and the giant.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 The beam      Fires top-to-bottom, hurting BOTH sides —
               stay out of the lane, bait foes into it.
 Advance       Field troops rush you; fight on your terms.
 Finish        Destroy Galshook and Bortz.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
