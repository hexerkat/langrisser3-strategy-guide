# Scenario 36: The Birth of a Legend
*The last battle against Böser*

> {{enf:scen112:19}}

Geier felled, the party enters the glowing inner sanctum where the Chaos ceremony
nears its end. **Böser** — Kaiser Paul, vessel of the chaos god — must fall before
the rite completes, or your beloved is lost and Chaos fully wakes.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Victory** | Defeat **Böser within 17 turns** |
| **Defeat** | Diehärte falls · the ceremony completes (your beloved is lost) |

## Order of battle

| Side | Units |
|---|---|
| **Allies** | Your full best-ending roster (incl. Altemüller, Varna) |

None.

## Walkthrough

The final battle, against the **17-turn clock**. The ceremony at the chamber's
heart is counting down to completion — drive straight for **Böser** and bring him
down in time. Spend nothing on the demon host you don't have to; every turn lost
brings the chaos god closer to full awakening and your beloved closer to being
lost forever. Strike fast, strike together, and end it.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 The clock     17 turns until the ceremony completes.
 Charge        Push directly to Böser; don't dawdle on
               the demon host.
 Finish        Defeat Böser in time to save your beloved
               and seal Chaos — the legend is born.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
