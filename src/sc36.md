# Scenario 36: The Birth of a Legend
*The last battle against Paul, the true Böser*

> {{enf:scen112:19}}

Geier felled, the party enters the glowing inner sanctum where the Chaos ceremony
nears its end. **Paul**, now the true **Böser**, holds your beloved hostage. He
must fall before the rite completes, or Chaos returns and she is lost.

## Walkthrough

The final battle, against the **17-turn clock**. The ceremony at the chamber's
heart is counting down to completion. Drive straight for **Paul** and bring him
down in time. Spend nothing on the demon host you don't have to. Miss the clock
and Chaos rises, your beloved is lost, and you must defeat both Paul and Chaos to
finish the game. Strike fast, strike together, and end it.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 The clock     17 turns until the ceremony completes.
 Charge        Push directly to Paul; don't dawdle on
               the demon host.
 Finish        Defeat Paul in time to save your beloved.
 Too late      Chaos returns; defeat Paul and Chaos.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
