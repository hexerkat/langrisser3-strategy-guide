## Walkthrough

Deep in the castle, the **two arch mages flanking the throne room cast meteor to
blow up the bridge** to their alcove, then snipe you while you fight Wilder — reach
them with **water or flying units** and kill them. The **two arch mages inside the
throne room cast confusion**, so take them out first as well, then face the king.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Bridge mages  They meteor the bridge — cross with water/
               fliers and silence them.
 Throne mages  Kill the confusion-casters first.
 Finish        Defeat King Wilder.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
