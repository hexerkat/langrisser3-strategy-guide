# Scenario 01: Assault on the Floating Castle
*The day the sky fortress fell*

> {{enf:scen042:27}}

On the morning of his investiture, Diehärte's dream of knighthood is
shattered: Field Marshal Altemüller's black-winged host descends on the Floating
Castle under cover of the storm. This opening battle is a tutorial, and it
cannot be won.

## Walkthrough

The victory line asks you to defeat all enemies, but the reinforcements never
stop. The scenario ends only when Altemüller breaks the **Magic Cannon**. You cannot
save the castle, so don't try to hold it. Treat the fight as free experience:
the rank-and-file cannot harm the crystal, so leave it undefended and farm kills,
especially the **Lv 1 Hawk Knights**, before the end.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Throughout     Reinforcements are endless. Do not try
                to clear the map or defend the crystal.
 Farming        Hunt the Lv 1 Hawk Knights for easy XP.
 Scenario ends  When Altemüller destroys the Magic
                Cannon, the castle falls.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
