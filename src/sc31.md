## Walkthrough

A single **bridge** crosses the water, and the enemy showers it with arrows and
spells as you advance. Lines of **spikes** block water units; fliers can cross, but
archers wait behind the spikes for them. This is the **climax of Freya's arc**: if
Diehärte reaches her **and you chose every *persuasion* option** in the earlier
scenarios, **Freya joins and the battle ends** — opening the road to the best
ending. Miss them and you must cut her down, and that road closes.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 The bridge    One crossing, raked by arrows and magic;
               fliers can leap the spikes (mind archers).
 Reach Freya   Diehärte beside her auto-persuades — she
               joins if her arc was completed.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
