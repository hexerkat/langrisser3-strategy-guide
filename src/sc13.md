*The guardian of the eastern gate*

## Walkthrough

This is an indoor fight, and **cavalry move poorly inside the temple** — lead with
infantry and fliers. Push to the **2nd room on the left wing** to find Jugler;
he joins once the battle is won.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Indoors       Cavalry are slowed; rely on foot and air.
 Find Jugler   2nd room, left wing of the temple.
 Finish        Clear every enemy; Jugler then joins.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
