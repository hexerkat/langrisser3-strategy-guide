# Stage ?4: The Mightiest Foe
*Altemüller's final request: an all-out duel*

> {{enf:scen120:19}}

With Altemüller saved and Alhazard sealed once more, the field marshal asks one
thing of Diehärte — a contest with nothing held back, the strongest fight in the
game.

## Unlocking ?4 (best-ending route)

After clearing **Scenario 35**, use scenario-select to **replay Scenario 34**.
This time **persuade *and then* defeat Altemüller**; in the conversation
afterward, choose the new line **"it was an easy win, so I'm fine."**

## Walkthrough

This is a pure test of strength against Altemüller at the height of his power — the
toughest battle the game offers. Field your best-developed party, bring your
strongest gear (the **{{en:fntsys10:132}}** and **{{en:fntsys10:72}}** from this very
map help), and give it everything, just as he asked.

## Branching dialogue

The decisions are the **safe code (33659033)** and **examining Altemüller's chest**,
both listed under Buried treasure.
