# Endings & the Best-Ending Route

Langrisser III has **two endings** — a *normal* ending and the *true* (best)
ending — each with **variations by which heroine** you win. This chapter pulls the
requirements together; for the per-choice love-index detail, see *The Heroine
System*.

## The two endings

| Ending | How it happens |
|---|---|
| **Normal** | The game ends after **Scenario 34**. |
| **True (best)** | Meeting the checklist in Scenario 34 opens **Scenarios 35 & 36**, the true finale. |

> On the true route your **chosen lover is kidnapped** and cannot be used in
> Scenarios 35–36 (story reasons); **Altemüller and Varna** join in her place.

## Reaching the true ending

Everything below must come together at **Scenario 34** (the crossroads):

**Prerequisites carried into Sc 34**
1. A **successful love confession** (offered after Scenario 33).
2. **Freya** persuaded and in your party (her arc closes in Scenario 31).
3. **Langrisser equipped** on Diehärte.

**Inside Scenario 34**
4. **Persuade Varna** with Emerick or Diehärte (move adjacent — automatic); **keep her alive**.
5. **Persuade Altemüller** with **Emerick** (move adjacent — automatic); **keep Emerick alive**.
6. **Defeat Altemüller with Diehärte wielding Langrisser.**

Do all six and Scenarios 35 & 36 open.

## Winning Freya (the extra requirement)

Freya joins **only** if you take every **persuasion** option across her arc — and
avoid attacking her or wiping out the field when she's present. The persuasion
beats, in order:

| Scenario | Persuasion action |
|---|---|
| **Sc 07** | Stand beside Freya / let her strike first; in the flashback choose **"I'm sorry."** |
| **Sc 14** | "Sheathe your blade, Freya" · "Ask her why she fights." |
| **Sc 16** | (talking to Freya) "To see you." |
| **Sc 17** | "Tell me what troubles you!" · "I don't want to fight you, Freya." · "Why go so far?" |
| **Sc 31** | Reach Freya with Diehärte — **auto-persuasion**; she joins if her arc is complete. |

> Throughout: **never have Diehärte attack Freya, and don't defeat the Dark Knight**
> in Sc 6/16 — each lowers her index. (See Sc 32/33 for a few more ± choices.)

Your ending's flavour follows **whom you confess to after Scenario 33** — and that
needs her love index high enough by then. Rough safe targets (per the 攻略データ):

| Freya | +8 |
| Sophia | +12 |
| Liffany | +13 |
| Tiaris | +13 |

## How your characters are remembered

Whatever ending you reach, the epilogue narrates **what becomes of each
character** afterward — one rises to a great knight, another to a famed mage,
and so on. Each fate is decided by that character's record at clear time:
**final level**, **total enemies defeated**, and **total retreats**. Roughly:

| Their record at clear | Their fate |
|---|---|
| Many kills · **zero retreats** | Great deeds — their name lives on in history |
| Few kills · **zero retreats** | A quiet, peaceful life ever after |
| Any kills · **one or more retreats** | Dies, or vanishes without a trace |
| Any kills · **many retreats** | Comes to a wretched end |

So if a character means something to you, **fight hard with them and never let
them retreat** — that is how you earn them a happy life after the war.

## After the credits

Once the ending and staff roll finish, **leave the screen alone** — wait without
pressing anything and the cast's **voice-actor free-talk** begins. Press a button
and you're bounced back to the title, so be patient. (See also the **Hero Select**
gag and **New Game+** in *Secrets & Cheats*.)
