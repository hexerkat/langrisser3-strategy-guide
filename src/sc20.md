## Walkthrough

The commander is the **Lv 47 swordsman at the bottom-right of the ship**. The whole
fight is a race to reach him inside 29 turns. The **archers** are the real obstacle
on the way, with long range and heavy hits against your advancing line, so
kill them first as you push through.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Advance       Push toward the bottom-right commander.
 Archers       Take them out first. They out-range and
               punish your advance.
 Finish        Down the Lv 47 swordsman within 29 turns.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
