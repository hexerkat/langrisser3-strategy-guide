*Into the depths of Velzeria Castle*

## Walkthrough

On turn 2 **Varna** appears from your start and **Anna** appears behind Böser as a
hostage. When you near Böser's pedestal he turns on Anna — and **Altemüller steps
in front of her to help you**. **Kill the two arch demons immediately**: together
with Böser they can cut Altemüller down. **Varna attacks on arrival**, so shield
her too. Langrisser seals Alhazard's power for the fight.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Turn 2        Varna joins; Anna held behind Böser.
 Near Böser    He attacks Anna; Altemüller intervenes.
 Priority      Kill the 2 arch demons fast (they + Böser
               can kill Altemüller). Protect Varna.
 Finish        Defeat Böser; Alhazard is sealed.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
