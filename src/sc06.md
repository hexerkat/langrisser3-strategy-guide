# Scenario 06 — Enemies on all Sides
*Turning two armies against each other*

> {{enf:scen052:19}}

At the Rigüler–Barral border, Diehärte executes Baron Torrand's plan: make the
Empire and Barral believe the other struck first, let them tear into each other,
and slip away in the confusion.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Victory** | Defeat all enemies — *or* set the two armies fighting and retreat all allies across the cliff |
| **Defeat** | Diehärte falls |
| **Watch out** | Stall too long and the **Dark Knight** appears |

## Walkthrough

You do **not** have to beat everyone. The green units are Rigüler, the blue are
Barral. Dash your fastest units up to **both** armies until they start arguing
among themselves, then fall back to your starting point to end the scenario — this
can be one of the quickest maps in the game. Dawdle and the **Dark Knight**
arrives, and that is a fight you do not want yet.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Race          Send fast units to provoke BOTH the green
               (Rigüler) and blue (Barral) forces.
 Trigger       Once they turn on each other, retreat all
               allies back across the cliff.
 Caution       Too slow → the Dark Knight joins. Avoid.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

## Branching dialogue

None. (Should you meet the **Dark Knight** here or in Scenario 16, do **not**
defeat them — it lowers Freya's love index, which you need for the best ending.)
