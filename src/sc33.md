## Walkthrough

The enemy **splits three ways**. Some rush the civilians, some rush you, and some
hold with Rag. **Emerick's troops move to shield the townsfolk**. On turn 8, or once
every monster but Rag is down, monster reinforcements appear at the bottom-right.
When you near Rag, the **Dark Knight arrives in the town to help defend it**. Emerick is strong but can be
overwhelmed, so support him; he joins once the battle is won.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Three fronts  Foes hit civilians, you, and Rag's guard.
 Allies        Emerick (and soon the Dark Knight) defend
               the town. Back them up.
 Finish        Defeat Worm Master Rag.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

## Branching dialogue

After the battle, **Diehärte may confess his love**. The list holds five women and
nine men; choose her name, then **"yes."** A **successful confession** (to the heroine whose index you
have raised) is **required for the best ending**. If **Freya** strikes Rag, Freya +1.
