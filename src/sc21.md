## Walkthrough

It looks daunting but is one of the easier maps: the enemy **cavalry and fliers
advance on you under archer and mage cover**. Rather than charging in, **hold a
defensive line** and let them break themselves on you, then counter.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Hold          Set a defensive line; don't charge.
 Counter       Let the cavalry/fliers come, then break
               them with the unit triangle.
 Finish        Mop up; down Emerick.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
