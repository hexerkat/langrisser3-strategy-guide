*Ransch Village, half a year after the fall*

> {{enf:scen046:23}}

**Grove** is raising the dead. Here the sorrowful swordsman **Gilbert** joins the

## Walkthrough

Grove **resurrects** his ghouls and ghost each time you wipe them out, up to
three times, so this is an excellent place to grind. Clear the undead three
times for experience, then let Gilbert close on Grove to end it. Keep your civilians screened; their deaths are a loss
condition.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Turn 1        Engage the undead; protect the civilians.
 Turn 2        Gilbert joins.
 Grind         Grove revives his minions three times.
               Farm XP before you finish him.
 Finish        Kill Grove to win.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
