## Walkthrough

Nearing Feraquea makes her **shift into a stronger form**, so advance deliberately
and let your **mages pick off the dark snipers** (long range, heavy damage) before
you close in. On **turn 3, two sphinxes spawn at your own starting point** to hit
you from behind — keep a rearguard.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Advance       Slow and steady; mages kill dark snipers.
 Turn 3        Sphinxes appear at your start — watch your
               rear.
 Finish        Close on Feraquea (she powers up) and end it.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
