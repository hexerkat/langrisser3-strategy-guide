# Scenario 02: Madness in Laffel
*Flight through a burning vice-capital*

> {{enf:scen044:23}}

With the Floating Castle lost, Diehärte carries the wounded Gerold to the vice
capital Laffel — only to find King Wilder of allied Barral has gone mad and
turned on Larcuss. There is no winning this fight; the goal is to escape south
with Layla and Tiaris alive.

| | |

| **Defeat** | Diehärte falls · Layla falls |

| **New ally** | Silver Wolf (arrives turn 6) |

## Walkthrough

Don't be fooled by the "defeat all enemies" line — you are outnumbered and
out-leveled. Run **south**. The turn-6 wave led by King Wilder spawns at the
top-left, so by then you should already be near the bottom and able to slip away
cleanly. The starting Lv 1 fighters and lords are free experience if you have a
spare turn or two before the chase begins.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Turns 1-5     Push south. Mop up the Lv 1 rabble for XP
               and shepherd the three civilians along.
 Turn 6        Silver Wolf joins; King Wilder's column
               appears top-left and chases.
 Goal          Get Diehärte, Layla and Tiaris onto the
               southern edge to end the scenario.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
