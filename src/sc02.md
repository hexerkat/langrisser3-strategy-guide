# Scenario 02: Madness in Laffel
*Flight through a burning vice-capital*

> {{enf:scen044:23}}

With the Floating Castle lost, Diehärte carries the wounded Gerold to the vice
capital Laffel, only to find that King Wilder of allied Barral has gone mad
and turned on Larcuss. There is no winning this fight; the goal is to escape south
with Layla and Tiaris alive.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Victory** | Diehärte, Layla and Tiaris reach the **south edge** of the map |
| **Defeat** | Diehärte falls · Layla falls |
| **King Wilder** | His host appears top-left once Dios falls or your party leaves by the south |
| **New ally** | Silver Wolf (arrives turn 5) |

## Walkthrough

Don't be fooled by the line asking you to destroy all enemies. You are
outnumbered and out-leveled. Run **south**. On turn 5 **Silver Wolf** and his men
arrive to help. Dios's occupation force is beatable with patience, and the Lv 1
fighters and lords are free experience. But once Dios falls (or your party leaves
by the south), King Wilder's main army appears at the top-left. You cannot fight
it yet, so escape south before it reaches you.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Turns 1-4     Push south. Mop up the Lv 1 rabble for XP
               and shepherd the three civilians along.
 Turn 5        Silver Wolf joins.
 Dios falls    King Wilder's column appears top-left
               and chases. Leave by the south.
 Goal          Get Diehärte, Layla and Tiaris onto the
               southern edge to end the scenario.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
