# Scenario 10: Ceremony of Scorching Heat
*Rescue Sophia before the ritual*

> {{enf:scen060:26}}

Beneath the full moon the Shika prepare to sacrifice **Sophia**. Beat their chief
**Keh Shikairo** in time and she joins you, but the demon general **Worm Master
Rag** is waiting in the wings. This is also where **secret Stage ?2** can be set in motion.

## Walkthrough

**Down Keh Shikairo within 19 turns** to free Sophia; rush him, since the timer
is the real threat. When he falls, **Worm Master Rag and his demons** appear (Rag
from your own starting point, Do Kahni a turn later at the bottom-right), and the
surviving Shika switch to your side and charge Rag. Sophia becomes playable next
to Keh Shikairo.

> **Sophia's love index:** in any battle with a secondary objective, clearing all
> *human* enemies instead of meeting that objective lowers it (demons are exempt).
> Her tell is *"{{en:scen021:92}}"* (Sophia −1).

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 ≤ Turn 19     Kill Keh Shikairo to rescue Sophia.
 Phase 2       Rag + demons spawn; freed Shika help you.
 Do Kahni      Joins as NPC one turn after Rag appears.
 Finish        Defeat Kah Shind or Worm Master Rag.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
