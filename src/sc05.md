# Scenario 05: Brilliant General Emerick
*Reaching Baron Torrand's manor*

> {{enf:scen050:19}}

Geier's tip sends the resourceful **General Emerick** to intercept Diehärte at
Baron Torrand's estate. The baron's daughter **Luna** fights at your side, and
from this scenario on, your conduct in battle quietly shapes **Luna's love
index** (see *The Heroine System*).

| | |
|---|---|

| **Defeat** | Diehärte falls · Baron Torrand falls |
| **Allies** | Luna, Baron Torrand and his men |

## Walkthrough

Emerick holds off attacking Torrand until every civilian has escaped, which buys
you time. But **Luna charges straight for the castle** and will be swarmed if you
don't keep pace. The **gladiators** flanking the castle hit hard enough to grind
your High Lords to pulp, so deal with them deliberately. Clearing fast also feeds
Luna's love index.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Opening       Escort the civilians out; Emerick waits.
 Keep pace     Follow Luna toward the castle so she is
               not overwhelmed alone.
 Flanks        Kill the east/west gladiators before they
               break your High Lords.
 Finish        Down Emerick and get everyone to the
               castle. Quick wins raise Luna's index.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

## Branching dialogue

No spoken-line choices; the item triggers above are the only decisions. From here
through Scenario 30, finishing battles quickly and meeting secondary objectives
nudges **Luna's love index** upward. Her line *"{{en:scen006:198}}"* after a
battle is the tell that it rose.
