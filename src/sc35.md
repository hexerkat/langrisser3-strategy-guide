# Scenario 35: The True Enemy
*Into Velzeria, with old foes as allies*

> {{enf:scen110:110}}

Reached only on the best-ending route. **Kaiser Paul**, possessed by the chaos god
**Chaos**, has turned **Geier** into a demon general. With Altemüller and Varna now
at his side, Diehärte marches on Velzeria.

## Walkthrough

The penultimate battle. **Geier**, corrupted into a demon general, leads the host
barring the way deeper into Velzeria. Lean on your now-formidable endgame party
and your new allies **Altemüller and Varna** to break through to him.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Push          Cut through the demon host with your full
               endgame army + Altemüller and Varna.
 Finish        Defeat Demon General Geier.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
