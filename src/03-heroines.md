# The Heroine System (Love Index)

Five heroines can become Diehärte's beloved: **Tiaris, Liffany, Luna, Sophia,**
and **Freya**. Your dialogue choices quietly raise or lower each one's **love
index**. Bank enough by the confession scene after **Scenario 33** and she can
become your partner — a successful confession (plus Freya recruited) is also the
gate to the **best ending**.

> **Keep her alive.** Letting a heroine fall in battle tends to cost love index.
> You also earn points during the Character Creation quiz (see Chapter 1).

**Rough targets** (accumulated love index needed for a confident confession):

| Heroine | Target |
|---|---|
| Tiaris | +13 |
| Liffany | +13 |
| Sophia | +12 |
| Luna | **+29** (the grind — see her notes) |
| Freya | +8 (but requires the persuasion chain) |

**Legend:** ◎ +2 · ○ +1 · × −1 · ×× −2 · (blank) no change.

## Freya *(special — recruited via a persuasion chain)*

Freya must be **persuaded**, not just liked. Across her battles, choose the
*persuasion* option, never attack her yourself, and don't wipe the field down to
only her. **Do not kill the Dark Knight** (possible in Sc. 6 & 16) — it lowers her
index.

## Cheat sheet — winning all five hearts

The 攻略データ's "love-them-all" route:

1. **Character Creation** — answer: God dwells *in the heavens* · saved by *a
   wealth of knowledge* · live *to protect the one I love* · love is *given
   mutually* · fight *to protect my loved ones*.
2. Always let rival characters take their **revenge kills** when offered.
3. Buy the **present for Luna**; earn her praise **15+ times**.
4. Take every **single-heroine ○** option, naturally.
5. For the shared choices, pick: Sc. 10 *don't overdo it, Sophia* · Sc. 14 *ask
   Tiaris* · Sc. 18 *it can't be helped…* · Sc. 19 *leave it to me!* · Sc. 21
   *agree* · Sc. 23 *Tiaris* · Sc. 24 *accept the truce* · Sc. 27 *we'll be fine*.
