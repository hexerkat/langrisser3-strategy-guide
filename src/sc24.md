# Scenario 24: The Field Marshal's Determination
*A three-way battle, and an unlikely truce*

> {{enf:scen088:21}}

Velzeria launches its full invasion, and the field becomes a three-way war between
Diehärte's army, the Empire, and the monster horde. Here **Altemüller** makes his
choice — and an old enemy becomes a temporary ally.

## Walkthrough

After 2-3 turns the monsters appear (from the top-left corner; fliers from the
mid-top forest) and **Altemüller proposes a truce**. Your reply doesn't change the
outcome — the truce happens either way (refuse, and King Sieghart agrees to it).
Once you answer, **Altemüller and his survivors turn NPC and fight alongside you**.
Let the two enemy sides wear each other down and clean up what's left.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Turn ~2-3     Monsters spawn top-left; Altemüller asks
               for a truce (answer freely — same result).
 Allies        Altemüller's force becomes NPC.
 Finish        Defeat the monster horde to win.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
