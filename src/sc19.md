## Walkthrough

**Bortz bolts for the airship from turn 1**, so don't get drawn into clearing the
rank-and-file — drive straight after him and cut him down before he boards. If he
reaches the ship, it's a loss.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Chase         Bortz runs for the airship immediately.
 Ignore        Don't waste turns on the small fry.
 Finish        Kill Bortz before he escapes.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
