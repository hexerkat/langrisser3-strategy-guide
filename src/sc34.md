## Walkthrough

Persuade **Varna** by moving **Emerick or Diehärte** beside her. She joins as an
NPC. The map itself is straightforward; once you reach the **top balcony**,
**Altemüller** appears with a fresh wave of monsters.

### Best-ending checklist (the crossroads)
**Before** Scenario 34 you must already have:
- a **successful love confession** (Sc 33),
- **Freya** in the party (persuaded by Sc 31),
- **Langrisser equipped**.

**During** Scenario 34:
1. **Persuade Varna** with Emerick or Diehärte (move adjacent, it is automatic).
   Do this **before** anyone goes near Altemüller.
2. **Keep Varna alive** after she joins.
3. **Persuade Altemüller** with **Emerick** (move adjacent, it is automatic).
4. **Keep Emerick alive.**
5. **Defeat Altemüller with Diehärte wielding Langrisser.**

Do all of this and **Scenarios 35 & 36 open**. (Consequences: Kirikaze leaves the
party, your beloved is kidnapped, and **Altemüller and Varna join** in their place.)

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Persuade      Varna via Emerick/Diehärte → NPC ally.
 Balcony       Altemüller + monsters appear up top.
 Best ending   Persuade Altemüller (Emerick), keep both
               alive, kill Altemüller with Langrisser.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

## Branching dialogue

The decisive actions are the **Varna and Altemüller persuasions** above. See
