# Scenario 16 — Call to Arms
*Drawing the Dark Knight away from Laffel*

> {{enf:scen072:21}}

To retake Laffel, the Dark Knight stationed there must first be lured off. A
diversionary strike on Roguil Fort is the bait — and an informer left in the city
is essential.

## Walkthrough

It looks fearsome but plays cleanly. Open with your **mages**, whose range tops
the enemy **archers and snipers**, then send **fliers** to flank the spellcasters.
Be patient — but not slow: drag the fight out and the **Dark Knight** joins, and
that is a fight to avoid (and never *win*, lest Freya's index fall).

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Soften        Mages out-range and kill archers/snipers.
 Flank         Fliers swing onto the enemy mages.
 Tempo         Push steadily; too slow → Dark Knight.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
