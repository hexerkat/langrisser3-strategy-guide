# Stage ?5 — Tale of a Gell Family
*The villain of the piece is… you*

A bonus joke stage in which Diehärte raids the quiet forest home of a complete
gell family — father, mother, sons and grandfather — and ends up looking very much
like the bad guy.

## Unlocking ?5

Available **after Scenario 25**: you need a **save at Scenario 26 or later**, then
pick Stage ?5 from the list via the **scenario-select cheat** (see *Secrets &
Cheats*).

## Walkthrough

A short, silly fight. The extra gells arrive from the top-middle on turn 2. The
twist: **once you kill the husband, the wife gains a huge power-up and a meteor
spell**, so she becomes the real threat. Note that **Tiaris begins with no MP** —
she spends it all during the opening — so don't count on her magic. Finish the
family off and the stage drops you back to the title screen.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Turn 2        Extra gells arrive top-middle.
 The widow     Killing the husband powers up the wife
               (meteor!) — save her for when you're ready.
 After         Clearing returns to the main menu.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
